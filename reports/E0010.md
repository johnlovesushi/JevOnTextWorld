## Summary

- **SFT solves C1**: 358 / 360 test episodes won (0.994 ± 0.010 over seeds) after one epoch on 1709 expert states.
- **GRPO from the base model did not learn C1 in 60 batches**: 8 / 360 (c) and 15 / 360 (d) on the test set, against
  4 / 120 for the base model. No seed of c or d is above the upper end of the base model's 95 % interval (0.083).
- **SFT → GRPO is the SFT model**: the start already wins every episode, so the advantages are zero and the updates
  are close to zero; 357 of the 360 test episodes of e are command-for-command identical to those of b.
- H7 does not hold. H8 holds by its wording but carries no information. H9 holds for the win rate and the AUC; the
  Brier score of d equals the constant baseline within 0.003 (below it for 2 of 3 seeds).

### Hypotheses

- **H7 — does not hold.** The upper end of a's interval is 0.083. c: 0.0167, 0.0333, 0.0167; no seed is above it,
  and the mean of c (0.0222) is below a's own rate (0.0333). The difference between a and c is 4 / 120 against
  8 / 360, which is within noise in either direction.
- **H8 — holds by the wording, without information.** max(SFT, GRPO) = SFT, 0.9944 ± 0.0096; e = 0.9917 ± 0.0083. The
  gap is 0.0028, less than one standard deviation of either. But e is not a different policy: 120 / 120, 120 / 120
  and 117 / 120 of its test episodes are identical to those of b for seeds 17, 23, 29, and the one extra loss of e
  (s29: 119 against 120) is one episode.
- **H9 — win rate: holds; AUC: holds; Brier: equal to the baseline.**
  - Win rate: d 0.0417 against c 0.0222; d is higher by 0.019, not lower.
  - `forecast_auc` of d: 0.718, 0.709, 0.690 (mean 0.706), all above 0.6.
  - `forecast_brier` of d against the constant baseline: 0.0358 < 0.0369, 0.0863 < 0.0888, 0.0419 > 0.0417; mean
    0.0547 against 0.0558. Below for two seeds, above for one; the largest gap is 0.0025. On the validation set
    it is above the baseline for all three seeds at both the selected and the last checkpoint.
  - With 3 to 9 wins per seed, AUC and Brier of d rest on very few positive episodes. AUC_t0 of d is 0.12–0.40
    (below 0.5): at the first step the forecast does not tell winnable episodes apart; the AUC over all steps
    comes from steps inside an episode.

### Interpretation

- C1 without traps is a supervised problem that 200 walkthroughs solve in one epoch. The base model's losses are
  mostly a wrong or repeated `cook` (87 of 116); after SFT that failure is gone.
- GRPO from the base model starts at about 1 win in 32 episodes. A group of 8 then usually has no win, and such a
  group gives no gradient: on average 0.4–0.8 of the 4 groups of a batch contain both outcomes in the first 10
  batches. c s17 is the only run whose training win rate rises steadily (0.022 → 0.106 over the six blocks), and
  its validation and test results do not show it (0.05 and 0.0167). I cannot tell from 60 batches whether longer
  training would take off; what the data shows is only that 1920 episodes are not enough.
- The c and d checkpoints were selected among validation results that differ by 1–4 wins in 80. The selection
  is therefore mostly noise, and the last checkpoints would likely give test numbers in the same range. I did not
  evaluate them on the test set (the issue asks for the selected checkpoints only).
- The comparison "d against c" says that the calibration term does not hurt, at a level where neither policy
  learned. It does not show what calibration does to a learning policy.

### Decisions needed

1. **GRPO from the base model does not learn on C1 within 60 batches.** Whether to try more batches, larger groups,
   or another start is for the planner; I started nothing.
2. **SFT → GRPO has no headroom on C1.** A level where SFT is below 1.0 is needed to test H8 in earnest; E0011 B
   (SFT model on C2–C4) will show whether such a level exists.
3. **Group e checkpoint** (note 3 above): accept `batch0010`, or read the rule strictly (e = b)?
4. **For E0011 B**: issue #30 asks for "the best-validation seed" of each method. b and e tie at 1.0 over all three
   seeds at the selected checkpoint, and c and d differ by one or two validation episodes. Without another
   instruction I would take the highest validation win rate and break ties by the lower seed: b s17 `epoch01`,
   c s17 `batch0020`, e s17 `batch0010`.
